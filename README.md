# 🎓 Universal UPSC Exam Preparation Command Center

A modern, responsive, multi-exam study tracking system and real-time countdown dashboard for all UPSC aspirants.

## 🌟 Supported UPSC Exam Templates

- 🏆 **UPSC Combined APFC + EPFO EO/AO CRT 2026** *(Combined Recruitment Test - Dec 19, 2026)*
- 🏛️ **UPSC APFC 2026** *(Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner - Dec 19, 2026)*
- ⚖️ **UPSC EPFO EO/AO 2026** *(Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer - Dec 19, 2026)*
- 👑 **UPSC CSE / IAS 2027** *(Civil Services Examination - May 23, 2027)*
- 🛡️ **UPSC CAPF AC 2027** *(Central Armed Police Forces Assistant Commandant - Aug 1, 2027)*
- ⚔️ **UPSC CDS 2027** *(Combined Defence Services - Apr 18, 2027)*
- ✏️ **Custom Exam Builder** *(Create custom plans for State PSC, SSC CGL, RBI Grade B, etc.)*

---

## 🔥 Key Features

- **⏳ Live Real-Time Countdowns**: Ticking days, hours, minutes, and seconds to Form Release & Exam Day.
- **📚 100% Official UPSC Notification Syllabuses**: Complete topic checklists with 4-stage revision tracking (`Concepts` ➔ `Rev 1` ➔ `Rev 2` ➔ `PYQs Solved`).
- **⏱️ Integrated Pomodoro Focus Timer**: 50m Focus, 10m Short Break, 20m Long Break with audio completion chimes.
- **📊 Study Effort Analytics**: Daily study hours logger & subject-wise time allocation bar charts.
- **🎯 Dynamic Daily Study Goals**: Switch goals dynamically from 4 to 12 hours/day (`[ 4h ]` `[ 6h ]` `[ 8h ]` `[ 10h ]` `[ 12h ]`).
- **📤 Export & Import Backup (JSON)**: Share your custom syllabus progress and logs with study partners or switch devices.
- **📱 100% Offline Single-File Support**: Open `upsc_all_exams_dashboard.html` directly in any browser on iPad, Tablet, Phone, or Laptop!
